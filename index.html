Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our fiber doors.

What are fiber doors made of?

Fiber doors are made from a composite material combining fiberglass and resin. This makes them incredibly strong, waterproof, and resistant to termites, moisture, and weather damage — unlike traditional wooden doors.

Are fiber doors better than wooden doors?

Yes, in many ways. Fiber doors are 100% waterproof, termite-proof, and don't warp or swell. They're also more durable, easier to maintain, and often more affordable than high-quality wooden doors. They're especially ideal for bathrooms and humid areas.

Do you offer custom sizes and designs?

Absolutely! We offer custom sizing and design services. Whether you need a specific dimension or a unique design, our team can create fiber doors tailored to your exact requirements.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes, we provide professional measurement and installation services. Our experienced team ensures your fiber doors are fitted perfectly for optimal performance and appearance.

We are located at Plot No. 42-A, Row No. 01, A Area, Liaquatabad, Karachi, Pakistan. You can easily visit our shop to see our fiber door collection in person.
